## Objective

Correct Emerald Poke Ball quantity decoding in the shared game-owned acquisition layer and keep the JS/Lua providers behaviorally aligned.

## Why This Task Exists

Raw Emerald `ItemSlot.quantity` values are encrypted with `SaveBlock2.encryptionKey`. Treating them as plaintext produced values above the normalized schema's maximum of 999 during the BizHawk proof. The fix is narrowly scoped to acquisition decoding and fail-closed handling of missing SaveBlock2/key data.

## Dependencies

`P05-T011` bag/Poke Ball acquisition and `P05-T014`'s separately active Libretro adapter task. This task does not depend on or modify the Libretro branch.

## Scope

- Decrypt the Poke Ball pocket's 16-bit raw quantities using the lower 16 bits of the 32-bit SaveBlock2 key.
- Keep `itemId` plaintext and preserve the existing quantity schema bound.
- Make JS and Lua acquisition return `bag: null` when SaveBlock2 or its key cannot be read.
- Correct the public-safe raw-memory fixture and add regression coverage.

## Non-Goals

No normalized-state/schema changes, clamping, broader bag-pocket support, proof-orchestrator changes, events, rules, actions, notifications, or real-ROM acceptance claim.

## Acceptance Criteria

- The fixture's encrypted Poke Ball quantities decode to 8, 3, and 1 while item IDs remain 4, 3, and 2.
- Missing SaveBlock2 pointer or unreadable key produces `bag: null`, never a zero-key fallback.
- Plaintext interpretation of the encrypted fixture quantity is shown to exceed 999 and the corrected value remains schema-valid.
- JS and Lua use the same 16-bit XOR semantics and raw layout.
- Existing downstream mapping and provider parity remain unchanged.

## Implementation Notes

Authoritative `pret/pokeemerald` evidence is recorded in the implementation comments and reviewed for this task: `include/global.h` places `SaveBlock2.encryptionKey` at `0xAC`; `src/item.c` reads and writes `ItemSlot.itemId` directly while applying `GetBagItemQuantity`/`SetBagItemQuantity` to the separate `u16 quantity` field; and `src/load_save.c` carries the SaveBlock2 key through bag save/load handling. The runtime pointer table begins at Emerald's known `gSaveBlock1Ptr` location `0x03005D8C`, with `gSaveBlock2Ptr` at the following 4-byte slot `0x03005D90`.

The JS reader catches unreadable pointer/key reads and leaves unrelated state fields available. The Lua reader uses protected calls for those optional SaveBlock2 reads. Neither path clamps a decoded quantity or substitutes a zero key.

The raw fixture stores `8 ^ 0x1A2B3C4D` low 16 bits (`15429`), `3 ^ key` (`15438`), and `1 ^ key` (`15436`) at the Poke Ball quantity addresses, while item IDs remain plaintext.
