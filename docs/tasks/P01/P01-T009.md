## Objective

Record a new multiplayer/rules direction — a future **Session Referee** capable of declaring, negotiating, verifying, and (where supported) enforcing the environment a multiplayer session runs under, plus a future reviewed game-modification registry — in canonical product-vision documentation, without implementing any schema or runtime code. Then, as a second increment, perform a repository-wide product-vision consistency audit to ensure all active documentation accurately reflects the full current vision (not just the newest addition) and clearly separates implemented from planned.

## Why This Task Exists

Requested directly as product-memory/documentation work: the owner identified this direction and wanted it captured in the canonical vision before it's lost to conversation history, consistent with `docs/decisions/0002-documentation-as-project-memory.md`'s rule that documentation, not chat transcripts, is project memory.
